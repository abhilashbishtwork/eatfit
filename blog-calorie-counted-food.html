What "calorie-counted" really means at Eatfit

Most of us don't want to weigh our food or log every bite. We just want to know that a meal is doing right by us. That's the idea behind calorie-counted food at Eatfit.

Portions you can trust

Every dish on our menu is built to a set recipe and a set portion. Our nutritionists approve each one, and our kitchens follow the same recipe every time. So the bowl you order on a Monday in Bengaluru is the same bowl you get on a Friday in Pune.

The numbers are on the app

When you order on Swiggy, Zomato or Ownly, look for the nutrition details on the dish. Use them the way you like: to stay within a daily target, to pick a lighter dinner after a heavy lunch, or simply to know what you're eating.

Counting calories doesn't mean cutting flavour

A calorie-counted meal should still be one you look forward to. That's why our menu runs from Creamy Pesto Paneer Bowls to Classic Rajma Chawal. Healthy made happier is the whole point.
