Why we don't deep fry, and what we do instead

Good food starts with what you leave out. At EatFit, a few rules apply to every dish, in every city.

Zero deep frying

We cook by roasting, grilling, steaming, sautéing and slow-simmering. Each one brings out flavour without soaking the food in oil.

Zero trans fats and zero chemicals

Trans fats are among the worst fats you can eat, so we keep them out of our kitchens entirely. We also skip artificial preservatives and use real ingredients you'd recognise.

Whole grains wherever they fit

Quinoa, millets and multigrain blends show up across our menu, from khichdi to bowls.

Kitchens you can trust

Our kitchens are ISO-certified, and we track our ingredients from farm to fork. That's how we keep every meal consistent, safe and honest.
